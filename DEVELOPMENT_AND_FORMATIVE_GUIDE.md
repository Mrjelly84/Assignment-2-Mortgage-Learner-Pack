# Assignment 2 development and formative guide

This resource helps organise your work. 

## What you can reasonably draft after Week 7 (22 October)

- **Section 1 – System Design and Decision Logic:** your Week 6 problem statement, proposed architecture/flow, assumptions, decision table and planned human-review output. Then connect it to the code you are developing.
- **Section 2 – Machine Learning Approach and Implementation:** your own interpretation of the provided dataset audit and baseline model, why you selected inputs/preprocessing/model, and explanation of how your project actually runs.
- **Section 3 – Testing and Evaluation:** start a short expected-versus-actual record of model results and logical rule tests as you implement them. Extend it during independent study.
- **Section 4 – Improvement and Final Evaluation:** identify a supported limitation and plan a relevant change, implement and retest it as you develop, then compare original and revised results.

## Preparing for Monday 2 November formative

Aim for a **substantive developmental draft** rather than a polished final submission. Use Canvas instructions for the actual formative upload. Suggested evidence to support useful feedback:

- [ ] A draft organised around the **four published report sections**.
- [ ] Problem, system diagram/flow and decision table based on Week 6.
- [ ] Runnable mortgage baseline, initial dataset investigation and explanations in your own words.
- [ ] Progress in converting the decision table into an actual advisory function and connecting it to the model score.
- [ ] Initial **model AND logical-system** testing: even incomplete, clearly show what is working, what failed and what remains.
- [ ] At least one possible evidence-informed improvement, with source/reasoning and a plan to compare before and after.
- [ ] Initial GitHub history, README, dependencies and clear statement of tutor starter versus original learner work.

If a component is incomplete, **say what is incomplete and what you will do next**.
